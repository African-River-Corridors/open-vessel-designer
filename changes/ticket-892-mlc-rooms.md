- Crew rooms to MLC 2006 A3.1 for a sea-reaching Service Area (issue #892): a `coastal-and-river` deckhouse now sizes each room to the greater of Maritime Labour Convention 2006 as amended (ILO consolidated text including the 2022 amendments) Standard A3.1 and ES-TRIN 2025/1 Chapter 15, and each row of the `rooms` block gains `governing` (`mlc`, `es-trin` or `both`). Applied: A3.1.6(a) headroom 2.03 m; A3.1.9(a), (d), (e) one individual sleeping room and berth each, at least 1.98 x 0.80 m; A3.1.9(f) rating cabin floor 4.5, 5.5 or 7.0 m2 by gross tonnage (under 3,000; under 10,000; 10,000 and over); A3.1.9(k) officer cabin 7.5, 8.5 or 10.0 m2 where no day room is provided; A3.1.9(m) a day room (ESTIMATE 6.0 m2) and the (f) floor for the master, chief engineer and chief navigating officer; A3.1.11(c) one toilet and one shower per six persons; A3.1.11(d) a washbasin in every sleeping room; A3.1.12 a hospital (ESTIMATE 7.0 m2, with the toilet, washbasin and shower of Guideline B3.1.8(4)) from 15 seafarers. Sleeping-room sizes that change: the rating cabin from 3.50 m2 to 4.5, 5.5 or 7.0 m2, and the officer cabin likewise. Washbasins go from one per four crew to one per berth. **No exemption is applied by default**: not the under-3,000 GT ones of A3.1.9(a), (h), (m), 10(a), 11(b) and 15, not A3.1.20 (under 200 GT), and not the relaxation of A3.1.12 for coastal trade; the rooms block and a warning say so. The interim floor of the 40 ft accommodation units is no longer a lower bound for a sea-reaching hull. The crew block gains `officers`, the berths held by officers; the gross tonnage band and the officers join the berths in the rooms loop. River-only rooms are unchanged. The `rooms` and `crew` blocks are outside the results hash: no stored fixture hash moved. Tiers move on small coastal self-propelled hulls: the 60 x 10 m crude and bauxite sweep cases go from 1 to 2 deckhouse tiers (110.7 to 162.0 m2 of accommodation floor over a 131.2 m2 tier), and the lightship, GT and draught of those hulls move with them; the coastal 90 x 14 sweep cases stay at 1 tier (floor 110.7 to 158.5 m2). The floor areas are worked at 2.00 m of headroom (cautious for area); a new check `deckhouse_clear_height` tests the tier height of 2.6 m less 0.5 m for deck structure and services (ESTIMATE) against the 2.03 m of A3.1.6(a). Owner-margin persons who perform officers' duties (the electro-technical officer, a cargo engineer) are sized as officers, and the rooms block says the GT it used and settles only when berths, officers, seafarers and GT band all match the crew block.
