- Fix (issue #1033): a default coastal LNG design on a narrow hull (110 x 11.4 m, nothing typed but the size) failed with "beam and depth must be positive". The Defaults Profile stands the tank tops 2 m above the deck, so the depth follows the tank, and a tank smaller than 2 m at a trial depth took the depth to zero or below. Such a size is now refused, with the reason named ("The depth that follows the tank would be ... m"), and the search picks another arrangement; a hull where none fits is reported infeasible, not as a crash. The 110 x 11.4 m design now picks one tank across of 8.95 m.
