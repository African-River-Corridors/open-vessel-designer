- `igc-2g` 4.0.0 (issue #913, part 2): IGC 3.2.4.1 and 3.2.4.2 are checked on the placed deckhouse openings of #912, one row for each opening, with the limit 4 % of L of the load line length (ICLL Regulation 3(1)(a)), not less than 3 m, not more than 5 m. The four rows `igc/3.2.4.1/front-openings`, `side-opening-distance`, `opening-setback` and `air-inlet-setback`, which read typed numbers against the length overall, are gone (a major version). Five rows replace them, all `verified: false`: `igc/3.2.4.1/no-opening-faces-cargo`, `igc/3.2.4.1/side-opening-setback-placed`, `igc/3.2.4.1/air-inlet-setback-placed`, `igc/3.2.4.2/windows-fixed` and `igc/3.2.4.2/wheelhouse-gas-tight`. IGC 3.2.4.2 is read strictly: no wheelhouse door may face the cargo area, gas-tight or not. A stern with no placed openings (a container-block stern) has the new rows `not evaluated`, never `pass`. The typed inputs `deckhouse_front_opening`, `deckhouse_side_opening_distance_m`, `entrance_distance_m` and `engine_room_vent_inlet_distance_m` stay (the inland class rows read two of them) but no longer feed an IGC row. The coastal gas default stack takes 4.0.0 as the highest built-in version; `igc-2g` 1.0.0 to 3.3.0 are unchanged, and a file that stores one keeps it. No hull number moves.
