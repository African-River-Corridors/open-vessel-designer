- Fix (issue #1039): a coastal design with a typed minimum freeboard (110 x 11.4 m, `min_freeboard_m` 5.0) failed with "design_depth_m and ukc_m must be non-negative". With a standing tank top, a candidate's draught cap is its own depth less the freeboard, and a depth below the freeboard took the cap below zero. Such a candidate is now refused with the cause named ("The depth ... m is less than the minimum freeboard ... m"), and the search goes on; the same holds for the damage refinement and the no-fit placeholder. A freeboard at or above the typed depth is still refused by the inputs, as before.
