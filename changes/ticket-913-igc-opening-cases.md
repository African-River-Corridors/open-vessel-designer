- Evaluation cases for the IGC 3.2.4 opening Rules (issue #913, part 3): a new case kind `openings` in `evaluation/`, with an independent oracle `oracle/openings_oracle.py` that works each verdict from the IGC text. 30 base cases sit on each limit (4 % of L, 3 m at least, 5 m at most) and 0.01 m under it, at L = 70, 100 and 140 m, for side entrances, air inlets, openable windows and wheelhouse doors not designed gas-tight; and cases for a front door, a gas-tight wheelhouse side door (passes), one not gas-tight (fails) and a gas-tight wheelhouse door on the front (fails, the strict reading of 3.2.4.2). Every number of every new row is noticed by a rule mutant. The harness-only trace entry (`stern_trace`) takes a list of openings and L. No engine verdict and no Rule Set change.
