- Manning Rule Sets and the crew model (issue #778, spec 0011 D5a, D13, D14, D16, 4.1): three new draft Rule Sets, each built from the tables of the notice with every value traced to a paragraph and page and every Rule unverified: `liberia-man-001` (Liberia Marine Notice MAN-001 Rev. 11/22, 12.2: the 13-column table by gross tonnage, power and unattended machinery, with 12.1.9 and the watch paragraphs 3.2.1 and 3.2.3), `uk-msn-1868` (UK MSN 1868 (M) Amendment 1, Annexes A and B: officers only) and `ccnr-rpn-19` (CCNR RPN edition 1 January 2026, 18.01, 19.02 and 19.03). A new `crew` block in the results of a self-propelled hull gives the minimum crew by role, the owner margin apart from it, the berths (the crew plus 2 spare berths), the Rule Set with its version and hash, and every choice made as a note. The UK guidance runs beside the Liberia scale as a cross-check on officers, and a difference over one person is flagged. Four new inputs: `crew` (none, the model works it; a typed crew overrides and a crew under the minimum is reported), `manning_rule_set` (`liberia-man-001` for a coastal Option, `ccnr-rpn-19` for a river-only one), `navigation_hours_per_day` (24 coastal, 14 river) and `unmanned_machinery_space` (true). Worked cases of spec 0011 4.1: the 140 x 29 m coastal LNG hull gives 13 and an 85 m inland Type G tanker in mode B gives 4. The Liberia grid has holes (no column for 750 to 3,000 kW above 5,000 GT, or without unattended machinery): the model takes the higher neighbour (spec 0011 4.1 step 6: the neighbour with more persons), and the results name the column used and the other neighbour. The 13 of the 140 x 29 m hull holds up to 8,000 GT; above it the higher neighbour gives 14. The power band of an electric drive takes the greater of the propulsion power and the total genset rating (D14). RPN 19.03 (pushers) is in the Rule Set and in `ovd_format::crew::rigid_convoy_crew`; no run selects it until the Formation reaches the model (wave 3). The block is outside the results hash and nothing reads it yet (the deckhouse rooms come with issue #780), so no saved Design moves.
