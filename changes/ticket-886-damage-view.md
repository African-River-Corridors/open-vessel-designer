- App (issue #886): a new "Damage stability" view in the Stability and Propulsion group. For each Rule Set that brings damage cases (IGC, ES-TRIN, NORMAM, ADN, MARPOL) it lists the cases of each loading condition (flooded Spaces, floating position, mean draught, maximum righting lever, range, area and verdict) and marks the governing case, as the Rule Trace shows it. A case with intermediate stages of flooding shows them. A gas Design with damage cases shows the damage case drawing of the governing case of the condition. Where the Rule Set has no damage cases, the view gives the reason the trace gives. The shell sends the damage drawings with the evaluation, only for an Option whose results hold them.
