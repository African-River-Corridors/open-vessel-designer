- Damage stability, phase 2 (issue #763, spec 0012 D2, D3): a new Rule Set `adn-gas` 1.0.0 lists every paragraph of ADN 2025 9.3.1 (type G, gas) and evaluates 9.3.1.15.1 and 9.3.1.15.2, the damage stability, on every IS Code loading condition, with the intermediate stages at 25, 50 and 75 %; every other paragraph is listed as not evaluated, with its reason. `es-trin-2025` 2.0.0 evaluates ES-TRIN 28.03(4) to (6) for a gas or liquid bulk craft over 110 m, and lists the heeling test, the openings and cross-flooding (28.03(2), (5)(c), (6)(a), (6)(d), (7) to (10)) as not evaluated. `normam-202` 2.0.0 evaluates NORMAM-202/DPC 5.23.8 and 5.23.9 on the laden departure of a liquid bulk hull, with the MARPOL Annex I 28.2 extents (the numbers are the same), the end bulkheads of the main engine room intact, and a full cargo tank at 0 % as 5.23.8 (f) says; its rules apply to a gross tonnage over 20 (new derived input `gross_tonnage_band`), and 5.23.7 (c), 5.23.8 (e) and (g) are listed as not evaluated. ES-TRIN 28.03 damages the full load departure only (28.03(3)). The cases of each rule are in the new unhashed `damage_by_rule` block, and each has its own object type and Measures. No liquid result moves, and `adn-gas` is not in a default stack. Saved designs that pin the earlier versions do not change. A gas hold with Type C tanks in it floods at the greater of 95 % of its net volume and 70 % of its gross volume (ES-TRIN 28.03(4)(d)).
