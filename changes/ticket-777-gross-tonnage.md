- Gross tonnage (issue #777, spec 0011 D15): every computed Design now shows its gross tonnage by ITC 1969 Annex I Regulation 3, GT = K1 V with K1 = 0.2 + 0.02 log10 V. V is the moulded hull volume to the deck plus the enclosed volume above the main deck of the stern block (40 ft units and wheelhouse); the deckhouse joins V with issue #779. A new input, `gross_tonnage` (none = computed), takes a typed GT that overrides it. The results get a `gross_tonnage` block (V by its parts, K1, computed, typed and used GT, the citation), and the Rule Trace a `measures` list with the new Measures `option.enclosed_volume`, `option.gross_tonnage_k1` and `option.gross_tonnage`. No Rule or result reads GT yet, so the block is outside the results hash: no saved Design moves.
