- Deckhouse openings placed (issue #912, spec 0011 D17): on a `deckhouse` stern the engine now places the openings of the house: an accommodation entrance on the aft end for each tier, the machinery entrance, the engine room air inlet and the engine air intake, an accommodation air inlet, wheelhouse doors and openable windows on the sides marked gas-tight, and a fixed wheelhouse front window (the default layout: positions, door height and count are ESTIMATES). A typed list, the new input `deckhouse_openings` (rows of `kind`, `space`, `face`, `x_m`, `y_m`, `z_m`, optionally `openable`, `gas_tight`, `tier`, `name`), stands in for it, wholly; a file saved before reads null. Each opening is in `stern_arrangement.openings` with its position, face, kind and space, its distance from the deckhouse front, whether it faces the cargo area and whether it stands in the IGC 3.2.4.1 zone (4 % of L, not less than 3 m, not more than 5 m, from the deckhouse front; MSC.370(93) 3.2.4.1 and 3.2.4.2). L is the load line length (IGC 1.2.31; ICLL Regulation 3(1)(a)): 96 % of the waterline length at 85 % of the depth, without the rudder-stock alternative. New Measures on a new object type `opening`: `opening.distance_from_front`, `opening.faces_cargo_area`, `opening.in_zone`, `opening.openable`, `opening.gas_tight`, `opening.kind`, `opening.space`, `opening.face`; and on the Option `deckhouse.icll_length` and `deckhouse.opening_zone`. No Rule changes here (issue #913), so the four `igc/3.2.4.1/*` rows still read their typed inputs. The block is outside the results hash (it sits in `stern_arrangement`), so no saved hash moved.
