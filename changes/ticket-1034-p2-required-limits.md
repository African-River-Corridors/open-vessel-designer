- River-only Options need a number for the draught limit and the air-draft limit (issue #1034): typed, or filled by a Vessel Class. There is no "no limit" for them. A new Option, `ovd fill --as-new`, `ovd run`, `ovd validate`, the app and Python `simulate` give one message that names the limit that is not set (`simulate` raises `TypeError`, also for a key given as `None`). `ovd new` and `ovd fill` take `--draught-limit <m>` and `--air-draft-limit <m>`, in metres above 0. Plain `ovd fill` of a hand-written file gives the same message for a limit that is left out, or typed or bare `null`. Only a limit stored as `null` marked `default` (a file saved before this rule) keeps its results and hash, with one validation warning. The app shows the two fields with no value and no "none" choice for a river-only Option. An air-draft limit above a typed bridge clearance gives a warning. Coastal-and-river Options are unchanged.
- A deckhouse wheelhouse lifts only when a fixed one would break the air-draft limit (issue #1034, the maintainer's answer of 2026-11-09). A typed limit no longer makes it elevating by itself: where the fixed wheelhouse meets the limit, it stays fixed and the design does not move. This moves two sweep designs against main. "sweep gas river-only deckhouse 140x29" (limit 40 m) now has a fixed wheelhouse: light air draft 24.29 m, was 16.52 m; 4 tiers, was 1; the lift column leaves the stern block; the tank inner diameter, freeboard, GM, boil-off and one structure section move with the hull. "sweep optional deckhouse_openings" loses its lift column (lift allowed 0.00 m, was 6.00 m, used 0.00 m both times) and its figures move in the third digit. The run's `wheelhouse_basis` reads `limit_not_binding` for a fixed wheelhouse under a limit.
