- Fix (issue #1038): an arrangement could be reported feasible while its own IGC 2.7 damage rows failed (60 x 8, 80 x 9 and 80 x 10 m default coastal designs). A later depth pass of a standing height reused the damage verdicts of an earlier pass on a lightship that had since moved. Now the answer's own damage run decides feasibility: an arrangement is never feasible with a failing IGC 2.7 row, and one whose search only ran out of damage runs is feasible when the answer's run passes. The kept verdicts are still reused (they save the runs of a later pass), and the winner is worked out again at its own basis; if it fails there, the next best candidate is, within the budget.
