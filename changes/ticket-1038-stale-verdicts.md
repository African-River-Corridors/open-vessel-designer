- Fix (issue #1038): an arrangement could be reported feasible while its own IGC 2.7 damage rows failed (60 x 8, 80 x 9 and 80 x 10 m default coastal designs). A later depth pass of a standing height reused the damage verdicts of an earlier pass on a lightship that had since moved. Now the answer's own damage run decides feasibility: an arrangement is never feasible with a failing IGC 2.7 row. A kept verdict is reused as it is only while the lightship and the cargo stand where they were (within 0.01 %); one that failed is reused anyway, and one that passed is worked out again unless the damage budget is gone. The three designs are feasible with no failing row.
