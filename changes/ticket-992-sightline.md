- Sightline and air draft over standing tanks (issue #992, phase 3): with `tank_top_above_deck_m` set, a fitted weather shield that stands higher than the tank domes (its top is the tank top plus `weather_shield_clearance_m`) is the high point of the cargo area. It is the obstruction of the sightline and the top of the air draft, and the highest point is reported as `weather_shield`. Without the input the dome top stands as before. `arrangement.above_deck` also reports `dome_top_above_deck_m`, and its `top_above_deck_m` is the highest of the shield, the dome and the tank top; `weather_shield_mass_t` is never negative zero.
- The depth that follows the tank is found once (issue #992): a probe stops before the costly stability stage and takes the arrangement with the most cargo among those that pass the cheap checks, and the run is done once with that depth, so the draught limit, the design draught and the installed power come from it. The depth the run gives is checked against the probe, and the run is repeated with that depth (at most twice more) when stability turned the probe's pick down. `arrangement.depth_passes` says how many runs it took. Each arrangement is capped by its own depth less the freeboard, not the depth the run started from. The draught limit floor of the depth also applies with `optimise_depth`.
