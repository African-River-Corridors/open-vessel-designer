- Tank top above the deck by default (issue #992, phase 5): a new gas Option now stands its tanks above the main deck, and the moulded depth follows the tank (the maintainer's decision of 2026-11-07). Coastal and river: `tank_top_above_deck_m` 2.0 m, with the weather shield (`weather_shield_thickness_mm` 6 mm). River only: the new input `tank_top_above_deck_od_fraction` 0.5 as the most the share may be (up to the tank's widest point, the deck at the tank axis), with no shield (`weather_shield_thickness_mm` 0, whose soft range now starts at 0). The three defaults are by Service Area in the Defaults Profile. A file saved before keeps none for the height and the share, and 6 mm for the shield, so its depth and its results are as they were; a typed `moulded_depth_m` wins over a default height with no warning (a typed height still warns). There is no calibration case for a coastal gas barge with its tanks standing above the deck; the 2.0 m is the maintainer's figure. The depth is the double bottom, the support and the tank diameter less the height, and never less than the draught limit plus the least freeboard. The stored `moulded_depth_m` of a new Option is the ratio default it started from; the engine's depth is `dims.depth_m`.
- Every new coastal gas Option moves: the depth falls and the tank diameter may grow (the depth no longer limits it), so the tank count, the cargo, the lightship, the draught, the air draft and the stability and damage results move, each from the standing height and the shield. A new river-only gas Option moves the same way, and loses its shield mass. The share search (river only): the search tries 0.5 first and steps down (0.375, 0.25, 0.125, none) only when the share above fails the intact checks; it then screens that share by damage, and the next share down too if it fails damage. The share chosen is in `arrangement.tank_top_above_deck_od_fraction` (the limit in `..._limit`), the height in `tank_top_above_deck_m`, and `ovd run` prints a line for it; the app shows both. At most two damage-screened searches share one damage budget (the limit of the search, 24 runs). The table below lists the moved designs of the design-impact case set.
- Files (issue #992): the current fixtures, the website gas example and the sensitivities example store `tank_top_above_deck_od_fraction` as none (default); their results do not move.
