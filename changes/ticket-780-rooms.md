- Rooms sized from the crew (issue #780, spec 0011 D5, section 2): the deckhouse floor now comes from a rooms table worked from the berths of the crew model, in place of the floor of the 40 ft accommodation units. Rooms are sized to ES-TRIN 2025/1 Chapter 15 (15.02(6), (8), (9), (10), 15.03(1), (3), 15.07(1)): one single cabin per berth of 3.50 m2 (7 m3 at 2.00 m headroom), a communal room and mess of 2 m2 a person and at least 8 m2, one toilet compartment of 1 m2 per six crew, and a corridor 0.60 m wide along the cabins. A new `rooms` block in the results lists each room with its count, area, clause, and whether the area is sourced, derived or an estimate (the galley, work-clothes room, washbasins, showers and one stairway are required but the text gives no floor area, so each has a stated ESTIMATE area, marked `estimate` with its working; a warning says so). Every berth has its own cabin in every mode: OVD's own cautious rule (ES-TRIN 15.02(10) allows two to a cabin); RPN 18.02(4), a derogation from (1) and (2) for modes A1 and A2, is mentioned as context only. MLC 2006 A3.1 is not applied: its text is not in the rule sources, so a hull whose Service Area reaches sea is sized to the ES-TRIN rooms with the interim floor as its lower bound, and the block and a warning say so. The crew needs the gross tonnage, which includes the deckhouse volume, so the run repeats until the berths stop changing. The `rooms` block is outside the results hash, like `crew`; no saved hash moved and no tier count moved in the sweep. The deckhouse still carries no outfit mass (issue #782).
