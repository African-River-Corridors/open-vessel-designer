- Blind Distance limit by Rule Set (issue #989): the limit the wheelhouse is sized to, in the arrangement search and in the Rule Trace alike, is read from the blocking Rules of the stack's Rule Sets (`blind_limits`), not from a constant. ES-TRIN 7.02(2), 250 m unladen, is in both default stacks; a coastal stack adds SOLAS V/22.1.1, two ship lengths or 500 m, whichever is less, light and laden, and the least limit of a condition binds. The wheelhouse lift now also meets the laden limit. The `solas-v22` Rule Set 2.0.0 makes the two blind-zone Rules block (1.0.0 and 1.1.0 warn, and stay as they were); a new Option takes 2.0.0. The sightline check and the tug's sightline notes name the Rule that sets the limit instead of citing ES-TRIN. Not modelled: the 10 degrees either side and the blind sectors of SOLAS V/22.1.2, the ES-TRIN supplies condition (half supplies), a ballast condition, and the ES-TRIN 25.01 exemption of a sea-going vessel.
