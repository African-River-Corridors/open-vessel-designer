- A forward wheelhouse (`wheelhouse_forward`, issue #994) is always checked for its Blind Distance, on the ES-TRIN limit when the Option types none: with the eye ahead of the cargo, only what stands ahead of it (the bow) counts, so no lift over the cargo is asked. The results also say what the wings see aft along the hull side (`wing_view`, `stern_arrangement.rear_view`): a warning-level check, and two new Measures, `wheelhouse.wing_sees_hull_side` and `wheelhouse.wing_short_by`. Cameras for the view aft (ES-TRIN 7.02(3)) stay an open item; none is credited. No other style moves.
