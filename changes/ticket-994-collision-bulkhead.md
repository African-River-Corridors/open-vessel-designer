- The forward house stands aft of the collision bulkhead (issue #994, rework). Every result reports the forward perpendicular and the collision bulkhead plane under `geometry` (`forward_perpendicular_x_m`, `collision_bulkhead_from_fp_m`, `collision_bulkhead_x_m`). A new input `collision_bulkhead_m` (none gives 0.04 L, ES-TRIN 2025/1 Article 3.03(1)(a)) sets its distance. The `wheelhouse_forward` house has its front at the plane, or 3 m inside the stem if that is further aft, and the cargo zone ends at its aft face; the cargo length this costs is reported, and the warning says why. A new check, "house aft of collision bulkhead", and four Rule rows in two new Rule Set versions: `es-trin-2025` 2.2.0 (three rows) and `igc-2g` 4.2.0 (one row). The new `geometry` keys change every stored results hash, so the fixtures, the website example and `examples/` are re-stamped. The 80 x 9 m forward-house design in `damage_refine_bounds` carries 294 t, not 475 t: its cargo zone is 0.68 m shorter and the tank search takes a smaller tank.
