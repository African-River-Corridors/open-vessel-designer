- IGC cargo vent exit Rules (issue #849): Rule Set `igc-2g` 3.2.0 adds `igc/8.2.10.3/vent-exit-height` (the exit at least B/3 or 6 m, whichever is the greater, above the weather deck), `igc/8.2.10.4/vent-exit-above-working-areas` (6 m above working areas and walkways) and `igc/8.2.11.1/vent-exit-distance` (at least B or 25 m, whichever is less, from the nearest air intake, outlet or opening). They read the vent mast of #822 through `vent.exit_above_deck`, `vent.exit_above_working_area` and `vent.distance_to_openings`. Each applies on `coastal-and-river` only and blocks. The allowance of 8.2.11.1 for ships under 90 m in length is in the row title and is not applied. Text read: IGC Code, resolution MSC.370(93), 8.2.10.3, 8.2.10.4 and 8.2.11.1 (annex p. 112); the six amendments read change neither 8.2.10 nor 8.2.11. All three rows are `unverified` until a second reader checks them. Boundary cases (just meets, just fails) of each row, on three beams, are in the evaluation harness (`kind: vent`). `igc-2g` 3.2.0 is the highest built-in version, so a new gas Option takes it by default; a file that stores an older `igc-2g` keeps it, and its stored results do not move. **A new gas Option on a short hull can now fail `igc/8.2.11.1/vent-exit-distance` and so block**: the mast stands at the forward end of the cargo area, as far from the openings aft as the hull allows, and is not placed to meet the distance.
- Cargo vent mast, rounding (issue #849): the exit height above the deck and above the working area are never reported below B/3 or 6 m, and 6 m, when those terms set the exit (a gap of up to 1e-9 m is read as the term; `distance_met` is now exact, as the Rule has no tolerance). The height was the exit less the datum, which could fall one rounding step under the term (9.666666666666664 for 9.666666666666667), and a Rule with no tolerance read the mast the engine placed as failing it. Gas Options whose exit height rounded under the term now report the term, so `vent_exit_above_deck_m` and the results hash of those Options move; the worked example `examples/gas-140x29-coastal-lng/` is regenerated and its stored hashes re-stamped from `ovd run --json` (`tools/refresh_examples.py`).
