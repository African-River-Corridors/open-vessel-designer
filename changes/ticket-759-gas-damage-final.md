- Gas damage stability, final stage (issue #759, spec 0012): every IS Code loading condition of a gas Option is damaged with the IGC 2.3 cases under the 2.6.1 standard, by lost buoyancy. An intact Type C tank keeps its buoyancy in a flooded hold; a breached tank loses its cargo, which leaves the condition (IGC 2.5.3); ballast floods by its filling. The unhashed `damage` block lists every case with its floating position, residual stability and a deck-edge test, and gives the governing case per condition with its righting lever after damage. In `igc-2g` 3.0.0, `igc/2.7/damage-survival` is evaluated per case (IGC 2.7.1.2 and 2.7.2.1); openings (2.7.1.1) and emergency power (2.7.2.2) are listed as not evaluated. Saved designs that pin `igc-2g` 2.0.0 do not change.
