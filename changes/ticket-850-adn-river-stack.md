- River-only gas barges take ADN 2025 9.3.1 as their design Rule Set (issue #850, part B of two; part A is `igc-tank-clearance` 1.0.0, #904). The default river-only gas stack is now `adn-gas`, `igc-tank-clearance`, `is2008-a`, `lr-inland-gas`, `es-trin-2025`, `ovd`: `igc-2g` is out, and its two IGC 2.4.1.2 rows stay (through `igc-tank-clearance`) as the tank clearance sizing rows. A coastal-and-river gas stack keeps `igc-2g` and has no `adn-gas` row. A river-only gas Option's trace therefore has no other `igc-2g` row (the IGC tank wall, steel, damage and opening rows leave it; the ADN damage rows of `adn-gas` take the place of the IGC damage rows).
- `adn-gas` 2.0.0 (major: the two catch-all rows `adn/9.3.1.11.2/hold-spaces-and-cargo` and `adn/9.3.1.22.3/cargo-tank-openings` are replaced by rows per requirement). New evaluated rows, all `unverified`, all `block`: `adn/9.3.1.11.2(a)/double-hull-width` (at least 0.80 m, Measure `tank.double_side_width`, sizes the hull), `adn/9.3.1.11.2(a)/double-bottom-height` (at least 0.60 m, Measure `tank.double_bottom_height`, sizes the hull), `adn/9.3.1.22.3/relief-valve-outlet-height` (outlet at least 2.00 m above the deck, `vent.exit_above_deck`) and `adn/9.3.1.22.3/relief-valve-outlet-distance` (at least 6.00 m from the accommodation, `vent.distance_to_accommodation`). The 1.00 m radius reduction of 9.3.1.22.3 is not applied. Not evaluated, with the reason: the saddles to 20 degrees below the tank centreline (the model has a support height, not a contact angle), the single-hull branch, and 9.3.1.11.2 (b) to (e). The rule id of the outlet rows is split in two (height, distance), because a Rule holds one requirement. Text read: ADN 2025 (ECE/TRANS/351) 9.3.1.11.2 (a) (printed p. 510) and 9.3.1.22.3 (printed p. 523). The two new Measures read the double-hull width (the clear side gap less the 0.6 m `wing_tank_gap_m`) and the double bottom height.
- Sizing (issue #850): the gas arrangement reads the 0.80 m and 0.60 m from the Rules of the stack, not from constants. The clear side gap is at least 0.80 m plus the wing gap (1.40 m by default) and the double bottom at least 0.60 m (a clear bottom height of 0.60 m plus the 0.5 m support), where IGC 2.4.1.2 asks for less; where it asks for more, IGC governs. A stack with no `adn-gas` 2.0.0 sizes as before. `simulate`, given no Rule Sets, now takes the stack `run` reads for a river-only gas Option that stores none, so the two agree on the same design. Every river-only gas Option with the default stack moves: tank diameter, cargo, lightship, draught and stability results change (the design-impact table lists the sweep cases).
- Files that move (issue #850): a gas file with no stored `rule_sets` and with no Service Area (read as river-only) or a river-only one is read with the ADN gas stack, the one the maintainer decided on. In this repository: `crates/ovd-format/tests/legacy/v1_adn_g_self_propelled.vdesign`, `v2_gas_lng_before_292.vdesign`, `v2_gas_lpg_before_292.vdesign`, `v2_gas_lpg_before_313.vdesign` and `website/assets/examples/lrg-gas-89.vdesign` (its stored results hash is re-stamped). A file that stores its Rule Sets (every file of format version 3 or later, such as `v3_gas_before_739.vdesign`, the fixtures and `examples/gas-140x29-coastal-lng/`) keeps them and does not move; a gas file that names the coastal-and-river Service Area and stores no Rule Sets keeps the IGC stack it was read with. The CLI test `json_results_are_what_simulate_answers_for_a_self_propelled_gas_hull` keeps its explicit IGC sets and its pins (2077.917858761847 t of cargo, 1521.630185591528 t lightship); only its file and its `simulate` call name the coastal-and-river Service Area, which keeps the IGC stack. A new test, `json_results_are_what_simulate_answers_for_a_river_only_gas_hull_on_the_adn_stack`, pins the same hull on a river-only Service Area with the ADN stack: `cargo_t` 1815.3019802248543 and `lightship_t` 1505.4984725541242, moved by the ADN 9.3.1.11.2 (a) floors. One sweep case becomes not feasible, and the Rule Set change explains it: the CN-derived LPG hull (130 x 17.2 m, `sweep CN-derived liquid 3`, which is an LPG gas hull) had two tanks across of 7.25 m outer diameter with 0.85 m of side clearance. ADN 9.3.1.11.2 (a) asks 0.80 m of wing, which with the 0.6 m wing gap is 1.40 m of clearance, so the two tanks across narrow to 6.70 m and the same volume needs 49.3 m of tank length: L/D is 7.41, over the 7 of ADN 9.3.1.11.1 (b) (pressure tanks), which the engine's length-over-diameter check applies. The one-across and three-across arrangements also fail, so no arrangement passes and the closest is shown. Boundary cases of the 9.3.1.22.3 rows (exactly on the limit, one float step under, outside) are in the evaluation harness (`kind: vent`, `vent_adn_river`).

- Old gas files, notes and tests (issue #850): opening such a file shows a note of its own (`msg-legacy-adn-stack`: it has no Rule Sets and, where it is missing, no Service Area, it is read with the ADN stack, and its results and hash move) in place of the "results are unchanged" note for those inputs. The three legacy gas files (`v2_gas_lng_before_292.vdesign`, `v2_gas_lpg_before_292.vdesign`, `v2_gas_lpg_before_313.vdesign`) are not changed: their stored hashes no longer match when they are opened, as for any real old file (`rule_sets_input.rs` asserts it). Also changed: `simulate`, given an explicit river-only Service Area and no Rule Sets, takes the stack a new gas Option stores (ADN 9.3.1, `igc-tank-clearance`, `is2008-a`, `lr-inland-gas`, `ovd`, at the latest versions; `es-trin-2025` is left out because its freeboard Rule reads Option inputs that `simulate` is not given), so its tank sizing agrees with `run` on a new design. A Service Area left out stays "not river-only" in `simulate`, so the Python API's results (pinned to the original Python) do not move; this differs from `run` and the Defaults Profile, which read a missing Service Area as river-only (since issue #925 it is an error). `run` reads an old gas file that stores no Rule Sets with the older set versions (`is2008-a` 1.0.0), as before. The `*_igc_stack` copies in `crates/ovd-format/tests/legacy/` hold the same three legacy gas files with the old IGC stack stored (and their old hashes), so the tests of their other inputs (dome, heads, stern, tug and so on) still prove those inputs leave results unchanged. A new fixture `gas_adn_river_two_wide.vdesign` keeps tanks side by side in the mesh sweeps. The wing row has a tolerance of 1e-9 m and reports the measured clearance less the gap. An ADN distance of the vent mast that a harness case does not give is not evaluated, not 0 m. Tests that need the IGC rows or the IGC damage cases now name the coastal-and-river Service Area or store the IGC stack; tests that pinned the river-only gas default stack pin the ADN one; the mesh sweeps keep their floors of 12 tanks.
