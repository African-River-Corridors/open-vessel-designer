- Deckhouse tiers are sized from what they hold, issue #943 (step 2 of 3). Room tiers hold the rooms table, one stairway for each tier above the first and a service allowance of 20 m2 (an estimate: casing, workshop, stores); they keep the full width and the front face, and are shortened from the aft end. Tiers that only lift a fixed wheelhouse (access tiers) are the wheelhouse plan and a stairway. Deckhouse area, volume, steel, cost and gross tonnage are sums over the tiers; the steel's centre of gravity is the volume centre of the tiers. The wheelhouse front and eye height are unchanged. Results gain `deckhouse.room_tiers`, `access_tiers` and `service_allowance_m2`. Lightship falls on river-only deckhouse designs; on gas designs with a deckhouse (the default arrangement for coastal gas since #937) cargo and lightship move too.
