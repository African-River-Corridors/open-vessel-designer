- The forward house of `wheelhouse_forward` (issue #994) has its own placed openings, read strictly against IGC 3.2.4: its aft end faces the cargo area, so no entrance or air inlet is there, the wheelhouse doors stand on the sides beyond the 4 % of L zone, and the entrances are on the fore face. The cargo vent mast now stands where it is as far as it can be from the openings of both houses, and its distance to the forward house is a new Measure (`vent.distance_to_forward_house`) and in the results (`vent_distance_to_forward_house_m`). New Rule Set versions: `igc-2g` 4.1.0 (three rows for the forward style: openable windows on the aft face, the 8.2.11.1 vent distance to the forward house, and the 3.2.5 A-60 boundary of the aft face as a not-evaluated note) and `ovd` 3.3.0 (the forward wheelhouse's Blind Distance check has its own Rule). New Options take them; saved files keep the versions they pin. No other style moves.
