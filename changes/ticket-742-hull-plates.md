- Hull steel (issue #742, spec 0013): a plate model, `hull_plates`, re-cuts the gas hull's panel model into plates, stiffeners and primary members. Seams fall across the girth at the stock plate width and butts along the length at the stock plate length, each kept 50 mm clear of a longitudinal or a frame (an Estimate). Each stiffener is one trace line per spacing; each primary member one item per frame. The rule-net thickness is the panel's; the ordered thickness rounds it up to the `thickness_step_mm` of the `structure` block. The scrap is counted: like pieces of one panel are cut side by side from stock plates of the `structure` block's size, and the scrap is the stock bought less the net plate area. The results carry a `structure` block (`steel_list`, `groups`, `purchase`) and the item geometry (`structure_geometry`), both outside the results hash until the lightship reads the plate model (spec 0013, T7). `ovd export --format steel-csv` writes the steel list. Lightening holes are not deducted, and the list says so. No result moves.
