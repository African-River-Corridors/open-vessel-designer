- Capital budget (issue #773, spec 0011 D9 and sec. 7): a self-propelled hull prices its own stern with four item rates after the propulsion line: gensets per kWe of installed rating (every set and the reserve), machinery steel per t (the stern block's engine-room steel and engine seats, 0 until the results hold them), deckhouse per m² (0 until the deckhouse model gives an area) and gas fuel system per kW (0 until the gas fuel system allowances give it). The defaults (500 per kWe, 2,500 per t, 3,000 per m², 200 per kW) are example rates with no source, and `cost.items` says so for each line. The propulsion rate keeps its value; its basis now reads propulsors, drives, motors and switchboard, and the gensets are their own line. A file saved before reads the new rates as 0 ("not priced"), so its budget does not move. The budget is not in the results hash: no stored hash moves.
