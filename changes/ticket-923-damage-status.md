- Damage status, issue #923: a run can skip damage stability, and its results and verdict say whether it ran. `ovd run --no-damage` skips it (the default still runs it). `results.json` gets `damage_status` (`run`, `not run`, `not evaluated` or `not applicable`) and `damage_cases` (the cases worked out, or skipped: a time estimate needs it); both are outside the results hash. The damage Rules of a skipped run read `not run`, a new outcome apart from `not evaluated`, and the verdict never says the design "meets every evaluated blocking Rule" then. The engine takes a progress hook (cases done and total, called on the calling thread, and it can cancel the run). The concept report states the status in its summary and in A.11. No result moves when damage runs. The Rule outcome `not run` is in the app's rule table and the damage view. Damage runs once per run: the propeller-sizing and deckhouse-rooms passes skip it.
